Keyboard and screen-reader setup

Individual cells receive real keyboard focus, with one cell in the Tab order. The alternative keeps focus on the grid. Try individual cells if your reader does not follow the selected square.

Letters and plus/minus shortcuts work only while the board is focused. Turn them off for speech input. Number entry, arrows, Shift plus Up/Down and the keypad remain available.

Escape switches between the selected grid cell and the Keyboard control heading at the top of the sidebar. “Grid active” or “Sidebar active” is announced first. Turn this shortcut off to leave Escape to your browser or screen reader. Tab always follows the normal page order.

Your screen reader may need focus or interaction mode to send keys to the grid. In browse mode, letters may navigate the page instead. Alternatively, activate a cell and use the labelled keypad buttons.

INFORMATION: How the audio works

Moving upward raises the row note; downward lowers it. Columns pan from left to right. With self-voicing, automatic coordinate speech is off by default. Press C on the board, use the Coordinates button, or enable Always speak coordinates to hear your position. Screen-reader labels always give both coordinates.

An edge gives a centred mid-pitched double tap. Edge messages are off by default; enable Show and announce edge messages under Built-in voice options to also receive text and spoken feedback in the selected announcement mode. Crossing between 2×3 boxes gives a short tick when enabled. A rising blip confirms an entry; a descending pair marks a clear. A rough descending pair marks a blocked action; the message explains why.

Row, column and box readings list six values, saying “blank” for empty cells. Screen-reader mode sends requested readings to a polite live region; self-voicing uses the built-in voice. Off makes no page announcement. The visible status displays the reading only when numbers are visible.

The welcome attempts to play on startup; if the browser requires interaction, it starts after your first key press or click. Tones start only after interaction. Built-in speech uses short chunks at your selected speaking speed and prefers a local English voice. The browser’s available voices may vary.

INFORMATION: Puzzles, undo and privacy

Random puzzles are generated inside this page. Clues are removed only while the solver finds exactly one solution. Locally legal guesses are allowed; only immediate row, column or box conflicts are blocked.

Undo and Redo retain up to 100 changes in this session, including resets and whole-puzzle replacements. They have no time limit. Preferences are unaffected by undo. Reloading clears puzzle history.

Board display offers four modes: show everything; hide numbers; hide numbers and grid; or hide numbers, grid and cursor. Whenever numbers are hidden, clue and entry shading is also removed, and the visible status line does not reveal cell values or coordinates. Fully hidden mode removes all cell highlights, including the blue keyboard-focus outline. A separate “Grid active” indicator shows when the board has focus without revealing a cell. Screen-reader labels, keyboard navigation and requested audio readings remain available in every mode. Sidebar and keypad focus indicators remain visible.

Preferences, including the announcement and focus modes, are stored only in this browser when permitted. Local-file and private-browsing storage may be unavailable or may not survive closing the browser.

The page has no external assets or upload code. A browser-provided network speech voice may send its utterance text to the browser’s speech service; use a local voice or avoid self-voicing when that matters.
